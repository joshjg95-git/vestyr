How I see it

Marketing that knows where it's going.

There's rarely a shortage of marketing ideas. The harder decision is knowing which ones deserve your attention, and being able to say why.

So I start from the commercial question rather than the channel: who is worth winning, what would make them choose you, and what marketing is actually responsible for. Those answers make everything downstream — the campaigns, the website, the sales materials — far easier to get right.

Deliberate choices about audience and positioning, made before more activity is added, are what let the work add up to something. Most of the time that's the whole job.

It's also the part I enjoy most: the line between the strategic decision and the creative detail. What a brand should stand for, the idea behind a campaign, the words on a page.

Typical reasons to get in touch

  • You want to focus marketing investment on the audiences and activity that matter most.

01  /  Positioning & messaging

Make your value matter to the right audience.

Strong positioning starts with choosing who matters most and what should matter to them.

I define the audiences to prioritise, the value your offer creates for them and the angle that makes it relevant — then turn that into a story and voice you can use across your website, campaigns and sales conversations.

Where this helps

  • The offer is genuinely complex and takes a while to explain.
  • Sales, product and marketing each describe the value differently.
  • The website says everything, so nothing in particular stands out.

What I can deliver

  • Positioning and value propositions.
  • Brand narrative, messaging and tone of voice.
  • Website copy and creative concepts that express the brand.

What this gives you

A shared explanation of your value, with words and ideas your team can use.

02  /  Marketing direction & priorities

Focus marketing where it can drive growth.

This is where we find your north: the audiences worth winning, the positioning that matters to them and the activity most likely to support commercial growth.

I connect marketing priorities to your business goals, sales reality and available resources. Then we decide what deserves attention, what can wait, and how each activity should contribute.

Where this helps

  • There are more good ideas than there is time or budget to run them.
  • Activity is happening, but it's hard to say what each piece is for.
  • Marketing and sales are working to different definitions of success.

What I can deliver

  • A review of your current marketing and the gaps that matter.
  • Priorities for audiences, channels, campaigns and budget.
  • An action plan with responsibilities and measures of progress.

What this gives you

A clear basis for deciding where to put your time and money, with every priority tied back to a commercial purpose.

03  /  Campaigns & sales materials

Turn direction into demand.

Once the direction is clear, I turn it into work designed to reach the right audience and create demand.

I stay involved from the initial brief through writing, creative development and delivery. Where the work needs specialist design, development or media support, I brief and coordinate it around the same objectives.

Where this helps

  • The strategy is agreed but nothing has been made from it yet.
  • Sales are rebuilding their own decks because the official ones don't land.
  • A launch is coming and the message needs to hold across every format.

What I can deliver

  • Campaign concepts, content and launch materials.
  • Landing-page copy and email sequences.
  • Sales presentations, product one-pagers and customer stories.

What this gives you

Finished materials built around a consistent message, ready to put your strategy into market.
